        About this app

        What it does

        Larkspur Air Support is a voice agent for a fictional airline. Travelers say their booking number, and it pulls up the trip, answers questions about the flight, and helps with seats and upgrades.

        It’s a demo. The travelers and bookings are made up, and no real flights change.

        Built with Deepgram speech-to-text and text-to-speech, Amazon Connect, and the Amazon Chime SDK.
